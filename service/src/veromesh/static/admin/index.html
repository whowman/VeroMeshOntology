Settings

When a steward records in a meeting what owners confirmed there, each owner is told what was recorded and until when they may object, with one reminder if they have not answered. When an agent's action needs an owner's approval (including a tool call the gateway holds), each consulted owner is told what the agent asks and why; in Teams they can approve or reject on the card. Every notice sent is on the decision log. Email uses the mail adapter set on the Usage page.

The systems agents act on through VeroMesh. Agents sign in to VeroMesh's MCP gateway (/gateway/mcp) instead of to these systems, and only the tools offered here reach them. A checked tool goes to the system only once the decision check allows it; one that needs an owner waits until they approve. A read-only tool passes through unchecked. A tool not offered is invisible to agents. Every change here is on the decision log.
